A group consisting of %%[[Faeleth Nightsong]], %% [[Otto Von Geist]], [[Thistle Nightingale]], and [[Willow Beaumont]].

Otto, Thistle, and Willow left [[Elmhollow]] in the summer of 1375, accompanied by [[Kirturok Stonehoof]], chasing after [[Juniper Beaumont]] who, wielding strange powers and wearing massive cursed armor, kidnapped town priest Sylas Beaumont.
