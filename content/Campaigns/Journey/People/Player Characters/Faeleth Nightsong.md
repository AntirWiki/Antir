# Description
Faeleth is a Wood [[Elf]] woman with long brown hair and slightly tan skin. Her green eyes are large with large irises, like most elves. She is typically garbed in furs and skins, but with a mix of elven craftsmanship. The only bit of gold on her is the circlet on her head.%%, which is the mark of the priesthood.%%
%%
# History
 Faeleth Nightsong was born 66 years ago in the wood elf settlement of [[Vernia]] in northern Antir, a small village of around 100 individuals. As with most of her kin, she was born to two parents but was raised by the village. She lived a generally uneventful life. She was raised to be a hunter, but she found herself gravitating to the priesthood of Soln, assisting the local priests with rituals, etc. 

Seven years prior to the current day, Faeleth was exposed to the presence of the Autumn Court. Something latched onto her that day, something otherworldly and nefarious. Some key changes:

- Faeleth is now almost entirely carnivorous. She can still eat plants, but gains little nourishment from them
- Faeleth is prone to bouts of extreme hunger and bloodlust. This is typically mitigated via hunting, but even then, she is periodically struck by an overpowering need to kill, rip, tear, and eat meat. This has on multiple occasions caused her to kill and eat someone close to her.
- Despite being both culturally and personally connected to Soln and Spring, Faeleth is now inextricably linked to the Autumn Court. This manifests in various ways, from attracting monsters and followers of Autumn to physically corrupting the area around her with its presence.

Travelling with her best friend, Faeleth saw red. When she finally came to, she looked down in horror at what she had done. Saelethil was dead, her body mutilated. Faeleth herself was covered in blood, on her hands, face, and body. 

Significant people from Faeleth’s life:

- Saelethil: Faeleth’s childhood best friend. Saelethil (say-luh-thill) was travelling with Faeleth on the day her life changed forever. Unfortunately for Saelethil, this was before Faeleth understood the extent of her curse and what it would do to her. As a result, she was Faeleth’s first victim. 
- Aimon: Faeleth’s twin brother. Following Saelethil’s death, Faeleth fled, unwilling and unable to return home and face what she has done. Aimon has not seen his sister in years, but presumably continues to search for her
- Priestess Pyria Wranfir: The Solnite priest in Faeleth’s village. A slightly older elf, well into her second century of life. She is very important to the culture of the village, and one of the few elves of her age to not have left for their waning period by that age.
