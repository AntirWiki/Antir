# Approaching the City
Abrail gives Thistle the bundle that contains the deceased Coppers' loot. 

>[!recite] Abrail
>"You should hold onto this. I'm not sure what's going to happen when we get to the city, but I think we're gonna be pretty distracted. Get it back to the guild, when you have a chance. It's the right thing to do for a comrade."


>[!recite] Halic
> The next day of travel is more somber than the one before. Even Abrail, who spent the first day boasting and laughing, seems focused on the road ahead. Around you, the trees start to thin, giving way to rolling hills dotted occasionally with fields and farmsteads.
> 
> Willow, at some point around noon you find yourself walking behind the rest of the group - not for the first time, since of the seven people you travel with, you're the least acclimated to this kind of long march. It's been getting easier, now that you've been on the road for over a week, but the first few days were nothing but blisters and cramps every time you stopped for the night.
> 
> Suddenly, you hear a voice. "Hey there." You look over, and then down, and discover that the halfling has appeared next to you. You didn't notice him at all until he spoke. "It's Willow, right? I'm Halic." He talks in a rural Athraithe accent not unlike your own.
> ...
> "I don't mean to pry, but it seems like this thing you're chasin' has somebody real important to ya."
> ...
> "I hope you find em safe. But you're on a real dangerous road, I'm sure I don't need to tell ya' that. Can I give you a bit of advice? Those three, walkin this road beside you... someday on this journey of yours, one a' them might be the only thing standin between you and the pale horse. In a life like this, if you can't trust the ones you're walkin' with, you can't trust nothin."

> 
> "So, I've gotta ask, whatcha got in that basket of yours?"

[[Zauberei]]

>[!recite] Approach
> Before long, you can see light reflecting off the Deonach river to the south. You reach it quickly, turning onto the road beside it. This road is far more populated than the one you left, and you're regularly passed by carts, and even barges on the river itself. As you've made your way further east, Autumn's chill has begun to creep into the air.
> 
> A few hours later, as you pass between two hills, the city of Zauberei suddenly appears before you. Most of you have seen the city of magic before, but Willow, you're struck by the sheer *immensity* of it. The city is circled by an earth-colored wall that could enclose *dozens* of towns Elmhollow's size. Behind it you see the occasional thatched roof and stone chimney, and based on the amount of smoke, there must be thousands of buildings inside. Further inside the city is a second wall, taller, made of shining white limestone. Beyond that, you can faintly see the tall white towers and blue roofs of Foxfire Academy, watching over the city.
> 
> There are even a few ramshackle buildings outside the walls, clustered up around the gatehouse
> 
> You can hear on the wind the sounds of the metropolis; the murmur of crowds, hoofbeats on cobblestone, and the distant ring of tools in use.

A gnarled gnomish man (Brint Hopplescop) hawks bad potions from a stall by the road - sells them as Minor Healing Potions for 2 gold, but they're really junk potions that deal 1d4 poison damage on consumption.

> [!recite] Earthwall
> Stepping through the gate, the sounds you heard from outside hit you full force. Just from here you can see hundreds of people moving about the streets as they work and shop and converse amongst the simple wooden buildings.
> 
> The sounds are accompanied by the smell of the city - the usual scent of people and animals, but under it are hints of coffee, spices, paper, and the faint ozone scent of magic.
> 
> Those of you who've visited before recognize the Earthwall district, the home of Zauberei's working class.

If anyone looks around carefully, there are a few people acting listless and staring vacantly into space. They stink, and inspection reveals them as ghouls.

Some locals saw a red-haired young person and an older main in a dirty white robe go through the gate to Whitewall. The guards there have vague memories of the pair - they were charmed.

The group are let through easily once Abrail's group show their guild badges

> [!recite] Whitewall
> The guard speaks up as you go. "Be wary. Something's just happened at the College, and the whole campus is in a fuss"
> 
> The noises of the city change with the scenery around you. The horseshoes step on paved flagstones instead of rough cobbles, loud haggling over prices is replaced by the argument of students in a nearby cafe, and the conversation is a quiet murmur instead of competing shouts.
> 
> The buildings here are taller and sturdier than those in Earthwall - much of the wood is replaced by stone or brick, and almost every building has glass windows and tiled roofs. Past those roofs, you see the tall white spires of Foxfire Academy not far away.
> 
> The smell of unwashed human and animal recedes, and the scents of coffee and magic become powerful.

# Foxfire
>[!recite] Foxfire Academy
>Foxfire campus is beautiful, full of pristine white walls and numerous displays of blatant magic - lamps that hover above the streets, towers defying all laws of architecture, and more - but it's difficult to admire the sights. People rush about in a panic - students and faculty fleeing the center of campus, and guards moving towards it.
>
>The center of campus is not hard to find - it holds the largest of the buildings by far, a wide tower that stretches so high you have to crane your neck to see the top. But it's shining walls are marred at its base - a gaping hole seems to be blasted into the side, almost 20 feet wide. In front of it stands an avian man with pale gray feathers giving hurried instructions to a group of guards. He seems to finish his orders, and the group spreads out around the tower - two of them incanting, then taking off into the sky.

# Dungeon
![[basementmap.png]]
- 4-5 **Shadow Wisps** that hide in the darkness and intentionally snuff out any lights that appear during the fight.
- An **Elite Luminous Ooze** that has been loosed from a containment tank and will chase the players as soon as it is discovered. The tank is still intact, and if the players can lure it in and close the door it will be once again trapped.
	- Drops a pair of **Farlight Stones** if defeated or captured

- A guard - human woman - has been sacrificed to the Autumn King, but she yet lives. There is fungus crawling its way through her body, which can be extracted with a DC 18 Medicine check or the ritual halted with a DC 18 Religion check. If she is not saved, Silas gains an additional second rank spell during his fight. 
	- She is held in place by a set of **Average manacles**

- Mages that are hiding and will attempt to attack the players at first sight, thinking they are among the attackers.
	- If the party talk them down, they offer two bottles of **bane oil (undead)** that they'd prepared to fight off the invaders

- A guardian enchantment on the wall leading up to the sealed door, with the effects of [Entombed Spirit](https://2e.aonprd.com/Hazards.aspx?ID=151).

Boss: Willow's father, risen from the dead as a **Deathless Acolyte** of the Autumn King. Accompanied by two Foxfire guards that he has raised as **Void Zombies**.
- He has a **staff** (see [[Journey Loot]])

He finds himself unable to harm Willow because of his deep-set fatherly instincts.

There are a number of doors in this room, all sealed. The one in the back, where Silas was standing, is covered in arcane seals, circles, and runes except for a symbol in the middle - the cat and crescent of the Catsi coven. If the door is touched, a voice - male, high pitched, with an Alturon accent - speaks in the minds of all nearby.

"The archmage has been alerted to your presence. If you are Valerie or Darios, please be patient and they'll be here shortly to let you in. Valerie, please don't break down the door. I know you can, but it takes time to replace, and neither of us want her to be left unprotected. If you are not Valerie or Darios, I suggest you begin crafting an excuse, because the archmage will likely not be happy when they arrive."

# Aftermath
The party return above to find Abrail, plate mail scrapped and sword shattered, heavily wounded and staring vacantly into space, tears running freely down her cheeks. 

>[!recite] Abrail's Account
>“It moved so fast… it went right past me, took down Emili first, and without her healing we couldn’t last long enough. Emyr was nearly cut in two, Halic got caught by that green fire… eventually I couldn’t stand up anymore, and it just… ignored me. Like it didnt matter if I was alive or dead, as long as I was out of the way. I think we hurt it pretty good, maybe we could have won if we were smarter…”

The General reports that just before Juniper emerged, ghouls appeared from nowhere and attacked across the city. While the guards and mages were distracted, Juniper emerged from the tower, book in hand, and vanished into the streets. Abrail was recovered, suffering from spell-shock, but the bodies of her companions weren't found.

### Archmage
The party are asked to stay nearby for a bit, as the Archmage will likely want to speak with them when she returns.

A few hours pass, the group having free reign of the academy and [[Zauberei]].

Eventually they are summoned to the office of Archmage [[Irina Varazs]], at the top of Foxfire Tower. Arriving, they are waved in by her assistant, a quiet dwarven woman name Janoura Biersby.

>[!recite] Entering the office
>The dwarven woman nods and gestures for you to enter. Through the door, you enter a room that can only be described as *arcane*. Every wall is covered in bookshelves, only about half of which contain books - the other half have an assortment of items unlike any you've ever seen. It's impossible to tell what's an instrument, artifact, tool, or just decor. There's an astrolabe with three slowly orbiting moons, a jade skull with a slowly pulsating light within it; there's a smoke-filled mirror on a stand, and as you glance at it, a pale hand emerges from the smoke, gently traces a single line on the glass, and vanishes again.
>
>The center of the room is taken up by a massive dark wood desk, even more cluttered than the bookshelves with tomes, papers, and knickknacks. Behind it, a tall half-orc woman watches you. She has greenish-tan skin and small tusks, with long dark hair flowing over her shoulders. Thistle, you would immediately recognize her as Archmage Irina Varazs, the most skilled Wizard in Foxfire - and possibly the world.
>
>There's a few chairs set out before the desk, but she does not wait for you to sit. (*Loudly*)"I'm told that you know about the thing that wrecked my library."

Irina asks for all the information they have on Juniper, and gives as much advice as she can. If the party imply that they're going after them, she warns them that Abrail's group were upper Silver rank, nearing gold. This means they were around level 9.

Irina pays the group 60 gold for their assistance in the basement.

"The second part of your payment is information. I don't know where that thing was going, but if I were to make an educated guess - and I'm well educated - it's on its way to Taluvhas to settle an old score. 

If you intend to follow it, and free your friend, I know a man who may be able to help. I recently helped an elven priest who calls himself [[Father Lament]] with work on sealing. I only saw part of his work, but I'm certain it would be useful to you.

I can give you a recommendation, but you'll have to find him first. That means you'll need to go to Alturon, and get into the Invisible Library. I won't be able to help you with that part. Trust me, I've tried."
