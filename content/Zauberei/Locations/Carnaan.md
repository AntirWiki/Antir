Carnaan is a large town in southern [[Zauberei]], at the foot of the eastern mountains near [[Strioc an Athraithe|Athraithe]].

The town is built up the side of a mountain, on a steep incline. The main roads through town are switchbacks to allow for a more gentle climb. The roofs of the buildings are painted in vibrant earth tones, making a remarkably beautiful view from a distance, especially at sunrise.

The town is mostly run by the **Amberheart Logging Company,** which operates the logging operations at the foot of the mountain as well as the mine at the top.

# Locations
The entrance to the **Mine** is at the top of town. The shafts and caves are known to be dangerous, either due to cave-ins or from monsters who tend to settle within (or emerge from below).

The **Logging Camp** is at the foot of the mountain and is the lowest part of town, surrounded by woods. Many of the Amberheart workers live in company housing near here, some of whom are carted up to the mine each morning.

**Cat's Cradle** is a pottery shop near the center of town. It is run by a pair of kind young human women, both dark skinned with light colored hair.


# People
The **Amberheart Logging Company**, which runs the logging and mining operations in town, is not kind to its workers.
