General Lie Konketsu is the commander of the Zauberan military and Vox Aegis on the city-state's ruling council. He is a [[Moondancer]]. He is from [[Owl Nation]], but he was cast out when he bore a half-human child. He originally joined the Zauberan army seeking retribution for this injustice, though he has since lost much of that fervor.

# Description
Lie resembles a Great Grey Owl, with dark grey feathers, a flat round face, and an eternally grim expression. 
%%
# History
Lie's mother was an ambassador, so he grew up exposed to the outside world more than most Moondancers. As a young adult, he met a visiting human adventurer, fell for her, and the two had a passionate - though brief - relationship. After she left, Lie laid an egg. Though he tried to keep his partner's identity a secret, it was revealed when the egg hatched a child with undeniably hybrid features. Lie named the child Ryuho, and the pair were cast out to prevent disgrace to Lie's high-status family.

Ryuho's mother left the Owl Nation long before his egg was laid, but Lie holds little resentment for that - though it has given him a somewhat low opinion of adventurers. "I know adventurers are allergic to responsibility, and she probably didn't even know it was possible for me to lay."

Ryuho is now a student in Foxfire, training to become a Magus.
