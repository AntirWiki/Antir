Soln is the god of nature and the hunt in [[Lucentia]].

They are said to be completely mute, and many members of their priesthood are as well - either by choice, birth, or injury.

Usually depicted as a tan-skinned Wood Elf

# History
Soln was originally a patron god of the [[Elf#Wood Elves|Wood Elves]] before they were integrated into the Lucentian Pantheon. Many wood elves still worship them alone, without paying any deference to the other Lucentian gods.
