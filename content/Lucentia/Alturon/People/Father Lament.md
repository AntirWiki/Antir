Father Lament is a priest who resides in the [[Invisible Library]] in [[Alturon]]. He spends his days studying possession, and perfecting new methods of exorcism and spiritual abjuration.

Lament does not dedicate himself to any single god, instead giving worship to the entire [[Lucentian Pantheon]].

# Description
Father Lament is a tall elf with pale gray skin and large light blue eyes. His hair is long and white, draping over the shoulders of his plain dark grey robes. His only adornment is an ever-present necklace of the Holy Star.

Complex tattoos on his wrists are sometimes visible under his robes. In truth, his entire body under the robes - and even his scalp under his hair - is covered in intricate wards and prayers designed to make possession of his body impossible.
