The Invisible Library is a repository of knowledge hidden in [[Alturon]]. Its contents include materials on fiends and fae, theogony and deicide, and more. It is hidden by a blessing of [[Rhylea]], and only approved priests and knights (or those with the Shroud Maiden's favor) are able to locate and enter it.

# People
[[Father Lament]] is a monk who resides in the library, researching methods of warding and combating against possession.
