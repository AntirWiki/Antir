A small village in northern [[Lucentia]], not far from the [[Silent Woods]]. Has a population of around 100 individuals, mostly  [[Elf#Wood Elves|Wood Elves]].

Most of the village follows the faith of [[Soln]], generally disregarding the rest of the Lucentian gods.

# People
**Priestess Pyria Wranfir** - The Solnite priest. A slightly older elf, well into her second century of life. She is very important to the culture of the village, and one of the few elves of her age to not have left for their waning period by that age.
