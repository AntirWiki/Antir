The Akashic Record is a library in Arcadia, the realm of fae. It is unfathomably large, and it said to contain within its walls recordings of all mortal knowledge. 

It is currently located parallel to [[Foxfire Academy]] in the mortal world. 

It is overseen by a librarian, an archfae with a head like a featureless equine skull and a long red robe that conceals innumerable hands. 

The library is often visited by academically inclined spirits of all kinds, and by those mortals powerful enough to walk between planes, such as experienced wizards and elder dragons.
