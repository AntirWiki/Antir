> [!recite] An Uncanny Fortune
> The tent you find yourself before is a dark, earthen red with eyes sewn in black across the panels. A faint smoke rises through a hole at its peak, and a sign shaped like a wagon's wheel proclaims, "Auspice and Augury found within. Learn the path of your heart." Faint music drifts out from the door.
>
Pushing aside the tent flap, the first thing that hits your senses is a wave of smoke. As your eyes adjust to the dim, flickering light, you see a campfire burning on the ground in the center of the tent. Behind it, you see a cloaked figure in a dark, wide-brimmed hat is sitting on the ground. Behind them, deeper into the tent, another individual with a red bandanna over her head plucks at a large stringed instrument. As the owner of the hat lifts their head, your eyes meet two points of faintly glowing red. 
>
"Well, howdy there partner," he says as he shuffles a deck of cards on the small lap table in front of him. "You interested in learnin' your fate?"

![[chord.png|right|184]]![[walker.png|right|200]] A pair of plane-walking fortune tellers in strange arcano-mechanical bodies. The pair are often found at festivals across Antir and beyond, peddling their fortunes to any who ask. They read fortunes with an unusual deck of Harrow cards.

**Walker** is the more talkative of the pair, and typically runs their tent. He wears rough, practical clothes and a southern-style hat.

**Chord** is enigmatic, hiding behind a poncho and a large round-brimmed hat. She's usually found playing guitar in the back of the tent, but works better with the cards and can seemingly tell when a reading is especially important.
